K2K is a planned 4–6 month, approximately 4,000 km walk from Kanyakumari to Kashmir. It combines endurance, travel, local conversations and long-form storytelling. Sponsorship helps turn the idea into a properly equipped expedition.

01 · Working budget
₹45KFood

₹55KGear

Backpack, tent, walking equipment and other essential expedition gear. Current working gear range: ₹50–60K.

₹20KSafety

Safety, basic medical needs, replacements and practical emergency expenses.

₹30KDocumentation

Storage, charging, media accessories, connectivity and the equipment needed to record the journey.

₹25KLogistics

Essential transport around route constraints, permissions, resupply and other expedition logistics.

₹25KContingency

A buffer for the things that inevitably happen when a human walks across a country for months.

₹2LTotal target

A working sponsorship target, subject to final quotations and the actual length of the expedition.

₹0Guaranteed outcome

No fake promises. Deliverables and usage rights will be agreed clearly with each partner.

02 · What the project creates
300+

Short-form videos / reels

Regular social storytelling from the road, covering walking days, people, places, problems and discoveries.

1000s

Photographs

A visual archive of landscapes, streets, food, work, people and the changing character of India.

DOZENS

Local conversations

Interviews and informal conversations with people encountered along the route, with tourism and culture stories where appropriate.

REGULAR

Written dispatches

Articles, field notes and newsletter-style updates that preserve the details a short video cannot.

FINAL

Book

The journey is intended to become a final long-form written record after the walking is complete.

03 · What a partner can support

More than a logo.

Potential partnership elements can include sponsor mentions, brand visibility on appropriate journey assets, selected content collaborations, destination-focused storytelling, content usage rights by agreement and speaking or community engagement opportunities.

The exact package should fit the partner. A footwear company, travel company, camera brand, tourism organisation and local business do not need the same partnership.
