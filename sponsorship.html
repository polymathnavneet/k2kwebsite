PARTNER WITH THE EXPEDITION · ₹2 LAKH TARGET

Put a story
on the road.

K2K is a planned 4–6 month, approximately 4,000 km walk from Kanyakumari to Kashmir. It combines endurance, travel, local conversations and long-form storytelling. A good partner does not simply put a logo on a page. They help make the expedition possible and become part of the story.

04 · The partnership rule

Fit beats noise.

A footwear brand, camera company, tourism organisation, hostel, food company and local business should not receive the same generic package. The partnership will be built around what actually makes sense for the project and the audience.

05 · What can be agreed

Clear deliverables.

Potential elements include sponsor mentions, branding on appropriate expedition assets, selected content collaborations, destination-focused stories, usage rights and speaking/community opportunities.

Everything is agreed before the partnership starts.
