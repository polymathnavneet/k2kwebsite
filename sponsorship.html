What the project creates

Content that travels with the walker.

300+ short-form videos and reels are planned across the journey, alongside written articles, longer YouTube videos, photographs and interviews with people along the route.

The project is also intended to become a final book, turning the road, conversations and observations into a lasting record.

Tourism-focused storytelling can also give destinations and local communities a direct place in the project, subject to the realities of the route.
