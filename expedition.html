HOW THE WALK IS SUPPOSED TO WORK

4,000 km.
One backpack.

The expedition is planned as a 4–6 month walking and storytelling project. The numbers are targets, not guarantees. The road gets a vote.

01 · Core plan
01Start

1 December 2026 at Kanyakumari.

02Distance

Approximately 4,000 km, depending on the final walking line.

03Duration

Expected 4–6 months, with recovery, logistics and route changes affecting the pace.

04Finish

Kashmir, after crossing the country on foot.

02 · Daily life

The unglamorous part.

Walking thousands of kilometres means the project is mostly made of ordinary days: waking up, packing, eating, walking, finding water, navigating, resting, charging devices, washing clothes, dealing with weather, recording what happened and doing it again.

The working food budget is around ₹300 per day. That is a planning number rather than a promise that every day will cost the same.

03 · Camp & sleep

Carry enough. Not everything.

The gear plan includes a backpacking setup and a tent, with camping used where practical and safe. Accommodation decisions will depend on the route, local hospitality, weather, safety and available facilities.

The gear budget has been estimated around ₹50,000–₹60,000, with the rest of the project budget allocated to food, safety, travel logistics and documentation.

04 · Documentation system
EVERY DAY

Field notes

Record the day's route, people, observations, costs, difficulties and memorable details before they disappear.

VIDEO

Short-form storytelling

The target is 300+ reels/short videos across the journey, with regular updates rather than waiting until the end.

LONG-FORM

YouTube and articles

Longer videos and written stories will provide context that a short reel cannot.

PHOTO

Thousands of photographs

A visual archive of the route, landscapes, food, work, streets, people and the changing character of India.

FINAL

A book

The completed journey is intended to become a long-form written record and book.

05 · Safety & navigation

Freedom needs systems.

Navigation will combine digital maps, route planning and local information. The public tracker is designed to show a published marker rather than expose a person's exact location continuously.

Weather, road conditions, fatigue and local safety information can override the planned route. A record attempt is never worth taking an unnecessary risk for.

This page describes the current project plan. Final equipment, emergency and medical procedures will be refined before departure.

06 · The budget

The current sponsorship target is ₹2 lakh.

The working plan prioritises food, gear, safety, documentation and the basic logistics that keep a person moving for months.
