THE PERSON BEHIND K2K

Navneet.

A young Indian writer who wants to understand a country by walking through it, listening to people and writing down what he finds.

Navneet · runner · writer
01 · The person

Walking is the next chapter.

Navneet writes fiction, travel pieces, personal essays and newsletters. His work circles around people, places, ideas, love, philosophy and the strange details of ordinary life.

He has worked on projects including The Secret Lover and The Jalebi of Rewa, and has built newsletter projects such as Letters by Navneet and writing focused on life and observation around BHU.

K2K is the point where that writing leaves the desk. Instead of imagining the road, he wants to walk it.

02 · The endurance side

The body has to carry the story.

Navneet has trained in running and calisthenics and has taken on long-distance running challenges. K2K is a much larger test: repeated days on foot, changing terrain, weather, food, sleep, logistics and the mental discipline of continuing when the novelty is gone.

4,000 km is not a content gimmick.

The walking itself is the central challenge. Everything else, from the videos to the book, is built around documenting it honestly.

03 · Why K2K?
01 · SLOW

To see India between the landmarks

A long walk exposes the spaces that normal travel compresses: villages, industrial edges, roadside shops, farms, neighbourhoods, small stations, tea stalls and changing landscapes.

02 · PEOPLE

To meet people without a schedule controlling every conversation

The project is built around local encounters. The goal is not to collect tourist attractions but to hear how people describe the places they actually live.

03 · STORY

To turn movement into literature

Every route eventually becomes a story if someone pays enough attention. K2K is an attempt to create that record in real time through writing, photography and video.

04 · PROOF

To find out what a young person can actually finish

The journey is also a personal experiment in discipline, uncertainty and endurance. There is a finish line, but there is no promise that the road will be comfortable.

04 · The ambition

Not just crossing India.

Navneet wants K2K to become a serious long-form record of the journey rather than a collection of isolated social posts. The intended output includes regular Instagram content, YouTube videos, articles, thousands of photographs, local interviews and eventually a book.

He also wants to pursue the distinction of being among the youngest people to complete a Kanyakumari-to-Kashmir walk. That is an ambition, not a claim of an already-awarded official record.

The important part is simpler: start at the southern tip, keep walking north, and finish in Kashmir.
