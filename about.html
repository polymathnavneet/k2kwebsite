ABOUT THE WALKER

Navneet.

A writer walking across India because some stories make more sense when you reach them on foot.

The idea

K2K is Navneet's attempt to cross India from Kanyakumari to Kashmir on foot. The journey is designed as both an endurance challenge and a long-form storytelling project.

The road will be documented through photographs, video, written dispatches and conversations with people along the route. The goal is not to collect tourist checkmarks. It is to pay attention.

The work

Navneet is a young Indian writer and storyteller interested in travel, culture, philosophy and ordinary human lives. He has worked on fiction, newsletters and digital storytelling.

K2K gives all of that a physical backbone: one route, one pair of feet, thousands of kilometres and a lot of roadside tea.

What K2K will produce
WRITING

Field dispatches

Short observations and longer essays from the road, with the final journey developed into a book.

VIDEO

Walking India

Regular short-form videos and longer documentary-style episodes showing the route, people and daily reality.

PHOTOGRAPHY

The visual record

Thousands of photographs documenting landscapes, streets, food, faces and the changing character of India.

PEOPLE

Conversations

Interviews and informal conversations with locals, travellers, workers, creators and anyone with a story worth hearing.
