THE LINE ACROSS INDIA

South
to north.

The working plan is a roughly 4,000 km walk beginning at Kanyakumari on 1 December 2026 and ending in Kashmir. The exact road is deliberately not frozen into a tourist itinerary. Safety, weather, road conditions, accommodation, local advice and the realities of walking will decide the final line.

01 · Working route corridor
START

Kanyakumari · Tamil Nadu

The southern starting point. The expedition is planned to begin here on 1 December 2026.

SOUTH

Tamil Nadu → Karnataka

The southern section carries the walk through Tamil Nadu and onward toward Karnataka. Working route notes include Kanyakumari, Salem, Madurai and Bengaluru, with the final sequence subject to route planning.

CENTRAL

Maharashtra → Madhya Pradesh

The journey then moves into central India. Long walking days, heat, water access, accommodation and road safety will matter more here than drawing a perfect line on a map months in advance.

NORTH

Rajasthan → northern India

The plan continues north through Rajasthan and onward toward the northern states. The exact corridor will be chosen as the expedition develops.

FINISH

Kashmir

The northern endpoint. The finish is Kashmir, while the final approach will depend on the safe and practical road available at the time.

02 · Planning principles

The route can move.

A GPS line drawn today cannot know tomorrow's roadwork, weather, local restrictions, fatigue or an unexpectedly helpful person who tells a walker about a safer road.

The destination cannot.

Kanyakumari is the start. Kashmir is the finish. Everything between those points is a working plan that must remain flexible enough for a real human being carrying a backpack.
