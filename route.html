Dates on

These are planning dates, not booked appointments. They are based on the current working corridor and an average of roughly 28 km per walking day. Weather, rest days, road safety, accommodation, detours and the actual route can move every date. Humanity has unfortunately failed to make roads perfectly predictable.

How to read these dates

Every date is an estimate. The route is a planning corridor, not a promise to walk through the exact city centre on that exact day. Once the expedition begins, the live GPS tracker and journal will become the source of truth for where I actually am.

02 · Why the plan stays flexible

Fixed start.

Kanyakumari is planned for 1 December 2026. That is the anchor around which the rest of the calendar is built.

Flexible road.

Road conditions, weather, fatigue, safe walking shoulders, accommodation and local advice can change the line. The goal is to finish the walk, not to obey a spreadsheet at the cost of common sense.
