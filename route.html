THE ROUTE · 4,000 KM

South
to north.

The route begins at Kanyakumari and heads north through India toward Kashmir. The exact day-by-day line can change with safety, weather, roads and the people met along the way.

Planned direction
START

Kanyakumari

The southern starting point. The walk begins here on 1 December.

SOUTH

Tamil Nadu → Karnataka

Moving north through the southern states, with the road shaped by local conditions rather than a rigid sightseeing itinerary.

CENTRAL

Maharashtra → Madhya Pradesh

The route continues through central India, where distance and weather become part of the daily calculation.

NORTH

Rajasthan → Kashmir

The final northern stretch ends in Kashmir, after roughly 4,000 kilometres on foot.

FINISH

Kashmir

The northern finish. The destination matters, but the record of the road is the real project.
