Section 04 — The walker

One
person

He has walked long distances before — the Konkan coast in 2021, the Brahmaputra from Pasighat down in 2023 — but never at this length and never with a camera he was obliged to feed every day.

The plan is unglamorous. Twenty kilometres a day, six days a week, one rest day taken wherever the road allows it. No support vehicle, no fixer, no pre-booked accommodation. Where the day ends is where he sleeps, and the film is whatever happens in between.

The camera is a single body with two lenses, charged from a panel on the pack. Every frame in the film is shot by the person walking, which is both the constraint and the reason for it.
