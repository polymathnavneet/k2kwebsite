The story

K2K is Navneet's planned long-distance walk from Kanyakumari to Kashmir, beginning 1 December 2026. It is an endurance project and a field-writing project: the road is the method, but the people and stories are the record.

01 · Quick facts

Walker Navneet Kumar, Indian writer and creator

Project Kanyakumari → Kashmir on foot

Start 1 December 2026 · Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu

Distance Approximately 4,000 km

Duration Planned 4–6 months

Base before departure Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh

Output Videos, photography, interviews, written dispatches and a final book

02 · Suggested description

Navneet is walking from the southern tip of India to Kashmir, carrying a backpack and a camera, and documenting what India looks like when crossed at walking speed.

This is not simply a route challenge. It is an attempt to collect ordinary conversations, roadside scenes, landscapes, food, work, weather and small human stories that are normally lost between destinations.
