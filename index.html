A life being walked.

Navneet is an Indian writer, storyteller and endurance-minded wanderer. He writes about people, places, ideas and the strange little moments that make a long journey worth remembering.

K2K brings those interests together. The plan is simple: walk, meet people, document honestly, and turn the road into stories, photographs, videos and eventually a book.
