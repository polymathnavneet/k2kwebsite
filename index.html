01 · Why walk India?

A country looks different at walking speed.

Planes are efficient. Trains are wonderful. Buses are useful. None of them let you spend a morning walking into a town, stopping for tea, talking to a stranger, watching a field change into a market, and then continuing because Kashmir is still thousands of steps away.

K2K is an endurance challenge, but endurance is only the skeleton. The real project is storytelling: to experience India slowly enough to notice it and to turn those encounters into writing, photography and film.

02 · The project
01Walk

Cover roughly 4,000 km from the southern tip of India to Kashmir, adapting the route to safety, roads, weather and reality.

02Document

Build a daily record through photographs, short videos, longer videos, written dispatches and conversations.

03Listen

Meet people outside the usual tourist circuit and record the ordinary stories that make the route human.

04Write

Turn the journey into a long-form body of work and a final book after the walking is finished.
