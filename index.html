01 · The mission

A country looks different at walking speed.

Planes are efficient. Trains are wonderful. Buses are useful. None of them let you spend a morning walking into a town, stopping for tea, talking to a stranger, watching a field change into a market, and then continuing because Kashmir is still thousands of steps away.

K2K is an endurance challenge, but endurance is only the skeleton. The real project is storytelling: to experience India slowly enough to notice it and turn those encounters into writing, photography and film.
